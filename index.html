Stuck at the same numbers?

After the first few years, progress stops being automatic. You train by feel, repeat the same weights for months, and hope something moves.

A notes app keeps a perfect record of it. It logs everything and never tells you what to do next.

How it works

Three steps, repeated every week of the cycle.

  1. 1

    Start a cycle from a template

    Pick a template and your training days. Hyperbro lays out a multi-week cycle: exercises, sets and starting targets.

    4 weeks4 daysUpper / Lower
  2. 2

    Log your sets

    Weight, reps, and how many more reps you could have done. That last number is called reps in reserve (RIR). An honest guess is enough.

    85 kg8 reps2 left in the tank
  3. 3

    Get next week's targets

    Hyperbro reads how each set went and sets next week's weight and reps automatically, so each week is a bit harder than the last.

    85 kg × 887.5 kg × 8

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, during early access. Paid plans will come later, and people who joined early will get a bonus when they launch.

Do I need to know what RIR is?

No. RIR means reps in reserve: how many more clean reps you could have done when you stopped the set. If you did 8 and had about 2 more in you, that's 2. A rough, honest guess is all the app needs, and it gets easier with practice.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. There's no account to create right now, and your training log stays on your device.
