I usually reply within a few days. For a bug, please include the app version (App Store → Hyperbro, or iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Hyperbro), your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you did right before it happened — a screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my training data stored?

Only on your phone, inside the app. There is no account and no cloud copy. See the Privacy Policy.

I deleted the app. Can I get my workouts back?

No. Deleting the app deletes its data, and there is no backup or sync yet. Please keep the app installed while you want to keep your history.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works without an internet connection.

Is Hyperbro free?

Yes, during early access.

Is this medical advice?

No. Hyperbro is a training log; its suggestions are calculated from what you log. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise programme, and stop if you feel pain or dizziness. Read the full health notice.
